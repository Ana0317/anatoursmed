Medellín · since 2018
Ana MaríaYour local host · ES / EN

"Colombia has a huge variety of fruits and stories that are little known abroad — and I love sharing them."

Ana María is a student and entrepreneur from Medellín who has been offering guided tours since 2018, sharing Colombia's lesser-known fruits, flavors, and stories. A fruit-snack maker at heart, she keeps every experience small — a maximum of 7 people per group — for an easygoing pace and real conversations with the locals who make Medellín special. Larger groups are welcome too — they simply book several slots across different times or days, so everyone still enjoys the same close, personal experience. Every tour also includes hotel pickup and drop-off.

Claustro Comfama Visit

Step inside a 200-year-old heritage building in the heart of downtown — once a convent, school, barracks, and jail, now a beautifully restored cultural landmark run by Comfama.

Inside: restored courtyards and corridors, the old chapel, a mural hidden under seven layers of paint, and remains of Medellín's oldest aqueduct.
